Four kinds of AI tickets. One clear next stop for each.

This article tells tier-1 agents how to sort tickets about Northstar's AI tools: what to check first, which priority to set, who it goes to, and how fast.

You don't need to know mortgage compliance. You need to know where each ticket goes.

Which tools this covers

This article covers every Northstar AI tool. Each tool adds its own section when it rolls out, and AI Enablement briefs the desk in a 30-minute session before launch. The Ad Review Assistant is the first tool live.

Ad Review Assistant LIVE Ad Review app COMING NEXT

Ad Review Assistant, in one paragraph

Loan officers and Marketing run draft ads through a Claude skill inside Northstar's Claude workspace. It returns a PASS, FLAG or HOLD pre-screen, offers fixes, and builds a submission packet. Power Automate puts the packet in the SharePoint Ad Review Queue and alerts Compliance in Teams. The assistant never approves an ad. Only a licensed Compliance reviewer does. It also refuses customer information, which is why the data incident type below exists.

Priorities and response times

Priority follows risk, not volume. One pasted borrower name outranks a broken upload for fifty people.

The four ticket types

Each type lists the checks to run, who it goes to, and what to tell the person. Copy-paste replies are on the Reply templates tab.

P1

1. Customer data pasted into a chat

AI Tools › Data incidentPortal: "Report an AI data incident"
CHECK AND ACT
  1. Thank them for reporting it. Say it plainly: no one is in trouble for reporting.
  2. Ask what kind of information it was (name, loan amount, address, SSN, account number), never the information itself.
  3. Ask which account: Northstar's Claude (signs in with Northstar SSO) or a personal account.
  4. Confirm the chat is deleted. If not, have them delete it while you're on the line.
  5. Ask whether the ad was submitted to the Ad Review Queue. If yes, note the Submission ID.
  6. Set P1. Assign to Compliance – Privacy & Incidents. Add Information Security. Add AI Enablement to the watch list.
Never ask them to paste, screenshot or forward the data so you can see it. That creates a second copy.
WHO DOES WHAT NEXT
Compliance decides whether it's reportable and logs it in the incident register.
Information Security confirms the chat is deleted in the admin audit log and checks retention settings. If a personal account was used, InfoSec leads.
AI Enablement counts it in the monthly trend and updates the Data Rules card or lab examples. No names.
Target: Compliance notified within 1 hour of the ticket opening.
Say: "Thank you for telling us right away. That's exactly what we ask. Compliance will follow up today. You don't need to do anything else."
P2

2. Access or sign-in

AI Tools › Access"I can't sign in" · "I don't see the Ad Review Assistant"
CHECK AND ACT
  1. Are they signing in to Claude with their Northstar email through SSO? A personal email won't show Northstar tools.
  2. In Entra ID, check the user is in the right group: SG-Claude-LoanOfficers, SG-Claude-Marketing or SG-Claude-Compliance.
  3. Added to a group today? SCIM sync can take up to 40 minutes. Ask them to sign out, sign back in, and start a new chat.
  4. Still missing after an hour? Check the user appears in the Claude admin console with a seat.
WHO DOES WHAT NEXT
AI Enablement (Platform Admin): missing seat, wrong group, or the plugin isn't assigned to the group.
Engineering – Identity: SSO errors, MFA loops, or an account that won't provision at all.
Target: fixed the same business day.
Say: "Please don't use a personal Claude account in the meantime. If an ad is due today, your branch AI champion can tell you the backup process."
P3

3. Tool not working

AI Tools › FaultErrors · uploads fail · report won't download · no Teams alert
CHECK AND ACT
  1. Check the Claude status page and the AI Enablement channel for a known issue. If there is one, link the ticket to it.
  2. Ask for the Submission ID (NHM-…), the time, and the exact error text.
  3. Uploads: is the file a PDF, PNG or JPG under 30 MB? Ask them to try a new chat and a different browser.
  4. Ask for a screenshot only if it shows no customer information.
  5. Sort by where it broke: in Claude (the review itself), or after submit (queue item, Teams alert, approval email).
WHO DOES WHAT NEXT
AI Enablement: errors in Claude, the skill not loading, a report that won't download, or a HOLD that reached the queue.
Engineering – M365 Apps: no queue item, no Teams alert, or a broken approval email. Copy AI Enablement, which builds these flows with Engineering.
Vendor support: only AI Enablement opens cases with Anthropic. Agents don't.
Target: within 1 business day.
P4

4. Wrong or missed flag

AI Tools › Feedback"It flagged something that's fine" · "It missed something"
CHECK AND ACT
  1. Confirm the tool did run and gave a report. If it didn't, this is type 3.
  2. Get the Submission ID and which finding they disagree with.
  3. Send the AI Enablement feedback form link with the Submission ID filled in.
  4. Resolve as Routed to feedback. Don't try to judge the verdict yourself.
If they ask "can I publish anyway?" PASS isn't approval, and a FLAG or HOLD isn't a final no. Only Compliance decides. They can skip a fix and submit, and the reviewer will see it.
WHO DOES WHAT NEXT
AI Enablement reads every form, acknowledges within 1 business day, and reviews patterns monthly with Compliance.
Real rule problems become rule change requests and ship at the monthly release after Compliance signs off.
"How do I word this better?" is a how-to question. Send them to the quick-start and their branch AI champion.

Not a ticket for us

"How do I use it?" or "Is this ad any good?"

Branch AI champion first, then the loan officer quick-start and the 30-minute lab.

"How does this rule apply?"

A Compliance question. Loan officers ask their champion, who takes it to Compliance. Reviewers ask the Compliance lead.

"When will my ad be approved?"

Reviewers decide within 1 business day, and items escalate to the Compliance lead at 2. The due date is on the Teams alert.

"Can I use ChatGPT or my own Claude?"

No. Only Northstar's Claude is approved for this work. Point them to the Data Rules card.

Who sits at each level

Most questions never reach the desk. The branch AI champion handles how-to. The desk sorts. Specialist teams fix.

FIRST STOP

Branch AI champion

How to use the tool, how to read a report, the resubmit prompt. Champions send real problems to the desk.

TIER 1

Service desk

Sorts every ticket with this article, sets priority, routes it, and sends the reply. Owns the ticket until it's assigned.

TIER 2

Resolver groups

Compliance – Privacy & Incidents · Information Security · AI Enablement (Platform Admin) · Engineering – Identity · Engineering – M365 Apps.

TIER 3

Vendor

AI Enablement opens Anthropic support cases for platform faults. The desk never contacts the vendor directly.
