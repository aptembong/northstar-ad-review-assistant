Data Rules for Ad Review

REQUIRED BEFORE YOUR FIRST LIVE REVIEW · 2-MINUTE READ

These five rules apply the Northstar AI Acceptable Use Policy to the Ad Review Assistant. They cover ad review only. The full policy still applies to everything else you do in Claude.

1

Never paste customer information.

No borrower names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, loan numbers, credit details, or a rate quote for a real person. Crop or cover them in flyers and screenshots before you attach anything. If the assistant sees customer information, it stops and asks you to remove it.
2

Know what's fine to use.

Your ad copy and images, your own name, NMLS ID and work contact details, Northstar company information, and sample or placeholder numbers.
3

Only use Northstar's Claude.

Sign in with your Northstar account. Never review Northstar ads in a personal Claude account or another AI tool.
4

A PASS is not approval.

Nothing is published until a licensed compliance reviewer approves it, and then only the exact approved version.
5

Report mistakes right away. No blame.

Pasted customer information?Stop. Open a service desk ticket marked "AI data incident" and copy Compliance. Then delete the chat.
Wrong flag or missed rule?Tell AI Enablement in the Ad Review feedback form, pinned in the AI Enablement Teams channel.
