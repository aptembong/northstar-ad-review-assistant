LOAN OFFICER QUICK-START

Check your ad before Compliance does.

The Ad Review Assistant pre-screens your social posts, emails, flyers and video scripts against Regulation Z and Northstar house standards. A review takes a few minutes.

Every flag tells you why, so you learn the rules as you go.

Your first review, step by step

Follow along with a sample: Liam Smith's refinance email to past clients. Each step shows what you do on the left and what Liam saw on the right.

Sample content. Rates, APRs and NMLS numbers are illustrative placeholders.

1

Start the review

Open a new chat in Claude. Attach your ad, then paste the starter prompt and fill in the blanks.

Review this [social post / email / flyer / website / video] ad for a [loan product] loan. Planned publish date: [date].

Works with pasted text, images (flyers, social graphics) and video scripts with still frames. Remove all customer information first.

LIAM'S PROMPT
refinance-email-draft.txt
Review this email ad for a 30-year fixed-rate refinance loan. Planned publish date: November 20, 2026.
2

Read your scorecard

You get a result for each of five areas. Your overall verdict is the strictest one. Below the scorecard: what's working, what the scan found, and a numbered action plan. Each item quotes your exact words, names the rule, and gives wording you can paste.

HOLDDon't publish as written. Fix it before you can submit.
FLAGNeeds a human look. Fix it, or submit with a note for the reviewer.
PASSNo issues found. Ready for compliance sign-off, not approved.

AreaResultWhy
Rates & APRPASS6.244% APR is stated for the 6.125% rate
Triggering Terms & DisclosuresPASS"30-year" is disclosed: 360 monthly payments, APR
Prohibited PracticesPASSNone found
House StandardsFLAG"Rates will never be this low again" is a pressure claim
Clarity & PlacementFLAGAPR only in the small footer, far from the rate

What's working: rate paired with an APR and an "as of" date; NMLS IDs and Equal Housing Lender present; a clear three-step call to action.

3

Pick your fixes

Claude asks which fixes to apply. Tap the ones you want, or All fixes. Nothing changes until you choose. Anything you skip stays on the list as an open item for the reviewer.

WHICH FIXES SHOULD I APPLY?
4

Check the revision

You'll see your changes marked up (removed words struck through, new words highlighted), then a clean copy ready to paste. Claude re-checks the new version automatically and shows the new scorecard.

Flyers and images: Claude can't edit your design file. You get corrected wording with placement notes, plus a mockup for your designer to finish.

LIAM'S MARKED-UP CHANGES

Today's 30-year fixed refinance rate starts at 6.125% (6.244% APR; see terms below) [1].

Rates will never be this low again, so now is the time to act. If rates have dropped since you got your loan, it may be worth a look. [2]

Verdict:FLAG→PASS
5

Submit to Compliance

When your ad scores PASS or FLAG, Claude asks, "Ready to submit this to Compliance?" Say yes and answer any last questions, such as the publish date.

You get a downloadable submission packet, and your ad goes into the Ad Review Queue with the status Submitted. Compliance gets a Teams alert automatically. You don't need to email anyone.

An ad at HOLD can't be submitted. Fix the HOLD items first.

The Submission ID is your ad's name from here on: NHM, the date, your initials, and your submission number for the day.

Step 6: After Compliance decides

A licensed compliance reviewer looks at every ad, including PASS. You'll hear back one of two ways.

Returned with comments

You get a Teams message with the reviewer's comments and a resubmit prompt ready to paste.

  1. Open a new chat in Claude. The original chat isn't needed.
  2. Paste the prompt from your Teams message, plus the reviewer's comments and your latest version:
Resubmit NHM-20261114-LS-1

Claude treats each comment as an action item, re-checks the ad, and updates the same queue item as version 2. If the Teams message gets buried, type the prompt yourself with your Submission ID.

Approved

The record is locked and you get a receipt email, with Marketing copied.

Company channels and email: Marketing publishes it. Emails go out from the marketing platform, signed by you.
Your own social accounts: you post it. Use the exact approved version, with no edits. Then select Mark as Published in your receipt email and add the live link.

Changed one word after approval? That's a new ad. Run it through the assistant again.

What it won't do

The assistant helps you get ready. People make the decisions.

Never approves an adPASS means "ready for sign-off." A licensed compliance reviewer approves every ad.
Never notifies anyoneIt stops at Submitted. Automated Teams alerts and emails handle everything after that.
Never accepts customer dataNo borrower names, addresses or loan numbers. If it sees any, it stops and asks you to remove them.

Quick answers

My ad got a PASS. Can I post it?

Not yet. Submit it. Every ad needs compliance sign-off, including PASS.

I disagree with a flag.

Skip that fix and submit. It stays open for the reviewer, who makes the call.

Can I check a video?

Yes. Paste the script or captions and attach a still frame for each on-screen disclosure.

Do I need my old chat to resubmit?

No. Start a new chat with your Submission ID. That's how the assistant finds your ad.
