Weekly pilot check-in

Thursdays, 30 minutes, in the AI Enablement Teams channel's meeting. Dec 10 and 17, then Jan 7, 14 and 21. Over the holidays the scorecard is posted in the channel instead.

Attendees: Akeyla Peele-Tembong (chair) · 2 pilot reviewers · Edmond and Atlanta branch managers or delegates · Marketing · Service Desk lead · Engineering when needed
5MIN

1. Safety first

Any P1s or near misses?

Every P1 gets reviewed: what happened, how fast it closed, what changes. No blame for reporting.

10MIN

2. The scorecard

This week vs last week vs the gate.

Returned %, pre-screened %, accuracy %, minutes per ad, decisions within 1 day, tickets. Focus on direction, not one week's number.

5MIN

3. What reviewers see

Misses and false flags.

Reviewers bring the "Missed an issue" and "Flagged something fine" notes, plus the top reasons ads went back.

5MIN

4. Loan officer voice

Feedback form themes and tickets.

What's confusing, what's slow, who is submitting without a pre-screen and why.

5MIN

5. Decisions and owners

Every action gets an owner and a date.

Rule fixes go into the next release. Notes are posted in the channel within 1 hour.

Top risks and how we respond

Five risks we expect, each with what we do ahead of time, the signal that tells us it's happening, and who acts. Reviewed at every check-in and monthly after go-live.

LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUMIMPACT: HIGH

1. Lab completion lags before go-live

Loan officers are busy. If the lab slips, branches go live with people who don't know how to read a report or what PASS means.

RESPONSE

  • Branch managers own readiness; it's on their monthly review
  • Champions offer 3 lab slots a week, plus the self-paced version
  • No exceptions: a branch under 80% moves to the next wave or the buffer
Signal: under 60% lab completion 2 weeks before go-live · Acts: branch manager, with Enablement
LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUMIMPACT: HIGH

2. Reviewers don't trust the pre-screen

If reviewers re-check everything from scratch, minutes per ad won't drop and the capacity gain never shows up.

RESPONSE

  • Reviewers rate accuracy on every decision, and their notes drive each release
  • Pilot reviewers see their own before-and-after minutes every week
  • Clear line: PASS means ready for review, never approved. Reviewers keep full judgment
Signal: minutes per ad above 15 after week 4, or accuracy under 85% · Acts: Compliance lead and Enablement
LIKELIHOOD: LOW–MEDIUMIMPACT: HIGH

3. Customer data gets pasted in

A loan officer drops a borrower's details into a draft, usually by copying from a real file.

RESPONSE

  • Data Rules acknowledged before access; the assistant refuses customer data
  • P1 path tested in a tabletop on Dec 3, before anyone has access
  • No-blame reporting, so mistakes get reported instead of hidden
Signal: any P1 · Acts: Service Desk → Compliance – Privacy + InfoSec within 1 hour
LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUMIMPACT: MEDIUM

4. Loan officers skip the pre-screen

Some keep emailing ads straight to Compliance out of habit, so reviewers get the old workload back.

RESPONSE

  • Queue items without a Submission ID are labeled "Not pre-screened"
  • Pre-screened % by branch is on every manager report; champions follow up
  • If still under 90% after Q2, Compliance decides whether to require a Submission ID
Signal: a branch under 80% pre-screened for 2 weeks · Acts: champion, then branch manager
LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUMIMPACT: MEDIUM

5. Spring volume spike or a rule change

Spring homebuying season and rate moves push ad volume up just as Wave 2 goes live. A regulation change could also need a fast rule update.

RESPONSE

  • Wave 2 is set for early March, ahead of peak season, with buffer to Mar 31
  • Urgent rule changes ship the same week, after re-testing and Compliance sign-off
  • Daily stand-ups in each wave's first week catch queue backlogs early
Signal: decisions within 1 business day under 85%, or a new regulation · Acts: Compliance lead and Enablement
