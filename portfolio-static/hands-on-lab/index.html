STEP 2 OF 7

Run the review 3 min

Attach the ad, fill in the starter prompt, and send. The assistant reads every word in the image and the caption, runs a rule scan, and scores five areas.

The starter prompt

Fill in the blanks the same way every time:

Review this [social post / email / flyer / website / video] ad for a [loan product] loan. Planned publish date: [date].

For Simone's post:

Review this social post ad for a 7/1 adjustable-rate purchase loan. Planned publish date: November 20, 2026.
Why name the product? The image says "FIXED," but the loan is an ARM. If you leave the product out, the assistant states its assumption, and it can't catch that mismatch as cleanly.

STEP 3 OF 7

Read the report 6 min

Start at the top: the verdict and one sentence on why. Then the scorecard, the scan, and the numbered action plan. Every finding quotes Simone's exact words and names the rule.

HOLD
Don't publish as written.
The 5.875% rate has no APR, "3% down" and "$1,450/mo" have no required terms, and the post calls an adjustable-rate loan "FIXED."

What's working

  • A clear, specific call to action: "DM me 'HOME' today."
  • The ARM is named in the fine print, so the product is known.
  • Strong brand look and an easy-to-read headline.

FACILITATOR GUIDE

Running the lab live in Teams

Thirty minutes, one sample ad, and everyone makes their own decisions. You keep the group moving and turn questions into feedback for AI Enablement.

LENGTH30 minutesBook 45 so there's time for questions.
GROUP SIZE6 to 15 loan officersAdd a co-host above 10 to watch the chat.
WHERETeams meetingYou share your screen and run the review in Northstar's Claude.
MATERIALSAd Review Lab folderSimone's image and caption, the rate sheet, and a link to this page.

Who leads

Two phases, so the lab gets better before it scales.

PHASE 1 · PILOT

AI Enablement leads

Enablement runs the first cohorts at two pilot branches, Edmond, OK and Atlanta, GA. After each session, we update the lab, this guide and the exit check based on what confused people.

PHASE 2 · AT SCALE

Branch AI champions lead

Each champion takes a 45-minute train-the-trainer session, co-leads one session with Enablement, then runs the lab for their branch. Enablement stays on call and reviews completion data monthly.

Before the session

One week before

  • Send the invite with this page linked.
  • Check the roster against Data Rules acknowledgments. Anyone missing completes it first.
  • Ask attendees to confirm the skill shows under Customize in Claude.

Fifteen minutes before

  • Open a fresh chat in Northstar's Claude. Never reuse one with real ads in it.
  • Download Simone's image and caption from the Ad Review Lab folder.
  • Open this page in a second tab as your backup.

Ground rules to say out loud

  • Sample content only. No real ads or client details today.
  • Nothing goes to Compliance. In the lab, "submit" is practice.
  • Wrong guesses are the point of the warm-up.

If something goes sideways

The live report's wording is different from this page.

That's normal. AI output varies a little from run to run. The verdict and the main findings should match. If the verdict doesn't match, keep going with this page and log it in the feedback form.

Claude is slow or down.

Switch to the self-paced steps on this page and run them on screen. The lab works the same.

Someone's skill is missing.

They follow along on your screen today and open a service desk ticket after the session.

Someone pastes a real ad or client detail.

Pause and walk through the data incident steps together: ticket, copy Compliance, delete the chat. Stay calm and kind. That's the habit you want.

A rule question you can't answer.

Say so. Write it down and send it to Compliance through Enablement. Never guess at a rule.

"Is this going to replace Compliance?"

No. It catches the common issues early, so reviewers can spend their time on the judgment calls.

After the session

Same day

Check that every attendee submitted the completion form. Send a short note with the quick-start guide and cheat sheet links.

Within two days

Send questions, confusing moments and the most-missed exit question to AI Enablement through the feedback form. Start the description with "Lab."

Monthly

Enablement reviews completions, readiness ratings and lab feedback, then updates the lab. You'll see the changes in the version note at the bottom of the lab home.
