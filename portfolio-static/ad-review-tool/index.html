Check your ad before Compliance does.

Pre-screens social posts, emails, flyers and video against Regulation Z and Northstar house standards. A licensed compliance reviewer still approves every ad.

Paste the reviewer's comments from your Teams message and your latest version below. The ad keeps its Submission ID and becomes the next version on the same queue item.
Data rule: no borrower names, addresses, loan numbers or other customer information. Crop or cover them first. If the assistant sees any, it stops.

What it won't do

  • Never approves an ad. PASS means ready for compliance sign-off.
  • Never notifies anyone. It stops at Submitted.
  • Never accepts customer data.
