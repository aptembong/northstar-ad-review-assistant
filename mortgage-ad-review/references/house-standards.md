# Northstar Harbor Mortgage house standards

Northstar Harbor Mortgage is a fictional company created for portfolio purposes. These standards show how an organization would add its own rules on top of the regulations. A real company would replace this file with standards from its own Compliance team.

Any missing item below → FLAG (unless a regulation is also broken, in which case → HOLD).

## Required on every ad
- [ ] Company name: "Northstar Harbor Mortgage"
- [ ] Company NMLS ID: "NMLS #0000000" (placeholder)
- [ ] Loan officer name and individual NMLS ID, when a loan officer is named
- [ ] "Equal Housing Lender" statement or logo
- [ ] For any rate or payment: "Rates and terms are subject to change and credit approval. Not a commitment to lend."
- [ ] For any rate: an "as of" date for the rate

## Brand and conduct
- [ ] Uses the approved Northstar logo (navy and gold), not stretched or recolored
- [ ] No guarantees: "guaranteed approval", "everyone qualifies", "no credit check"
- [ ] No pressure claims: "rates will never be this low again", "today only" (unless a real, documented offer)
- [ ] No borrower stories or testimonials unless documented consent is on file (FLAG for the reviewer)
- [ ] Images show a diverse range of people and homes; none suggest a preference for or against any group
- [ ] No customer information of any kind

## Channel notes
- **Social posts:** if the platform cuts off the text, the required disclosures must appear in the visible part or in the image itself.
- **Video:** disclosures must stay on screen long enough to read. Provide frames that show each disclosure.
- **Email:** disclosures in the body, not only in a linked page.
